## Acceptance criteria

- Motion blur is visibly vector-based (camera pans/rotations smear along screen
  motion, not a uniform radial blur) and can be turned off/on and tuned live
  without a restart.
- At least two dynamic lights can be placed/colored independently mid-game in
  the PTC; changing the count and color is visible immediately.
- Shadows can be toggled; with shadows on, a moving object casts a visible
  shadow from a dynamic light, and toggling off removes it.
- The light-probe grid density/resolution can be changed live; debug spheres
  render at probe positions and show their captured state.
- All of it is reachable from adb and from a new debug sub-tab.
- A PTC smoke test demonstrates the whole set and reports a pass/fail summary.
- No regression in the existing ninja chain, portal/momentum tests or the
  stereo render path.
